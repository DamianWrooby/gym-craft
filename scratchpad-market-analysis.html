Competitive Field Report PREPARED FOR: GYMCRAFT · AUG 2026 · INTERNAL

Who else is
coaching the data?

A critical read of the running- and fitness-analytics market — and the narrow ground where a one-person hobby product can still win.

Bottom line, up front

  1. Your analytics are already free elsewhere. TRIMP, ACWR, monotony and strain are the whole point of intervals.icu and Runalyze — both free — and Garmin Connect shows training load natively. This is table stakes, not a moat.
  2. Strava overlaps you less than it looks — for now. Athlete Intelligence writes one-way auto-summaries on each activity. Strava has no coach-style weekly report, and true "ask about this workout" only works by leaving Strava for Claude via the MCP connector (June 2026). Your interactive report and in-app run Q&A are not yet replicated in-app — but Strava owns the data and owns Runna, so this is the long-term watch-item.
  3. Your real edge is being a hybrid, and being cheap. Almost nobody does gym + running in one opinionated app. A €25 lifetime price undercuts every $15–20/mo subscription in the market.
  4. Direction: stop competing on analytics depth. Lean into hybrid athletes, Garmin-direct access (no Strava required), correctness, and price. Do not try to out-coach Runna or out-chart intervals.icu.

Where GymCraft actually wins — and where it is exposed

Honest inventory. The left column is defensible today. The right column is where the market is moving against a one-person product.

Real edge

  • Hybrid in one appGym plans and running load in a single opinionated product. Almost nobody occupies this cleanly — Fitbod is gym-only, Runna is run-only, Garmin does not coach.
  • Garmin-direct, no Strava taxYou sync Garmin activities without routing through Strava. As Strava absorbs the AI-coaching layer, "works without Strava" becomes a genuine positioning wedge.
  • Price no subscriber can match€4/mo, €36/yr, or €25 lifetime against a market of $15–20/mo. Lifetime pricing is a story competitors structurally cannot copy.
  • Weekly report + in-app run Q&AStrava has no coach-style weekly report, and its "ask about a workout" only works by leaving for Claude. Both of your AI surfaces live inside the app — a real, current gap in the incumbent.
  • Correctness as a featureAll-modality load vs running-only volume, "walks are not training," honest metric definitions. Rigor most apps blur. Marketable trust.

Exposed

  • Commodity analyticsTRIMP / ACWR / monotony / strain are free on intervals.icu and Runalyze and native on Garmin. Not a differentiator on their own.
  • AI narrative is being commoditizedStrava's auto-summaries and the Claude MCP route point at where the platform is heading. Not there yet in-app, but the direction erodes your AI story over time.
  • No daily adaptationTrainAsONE and Fitbod re-plan from what you did yesterday. GymCraft generates, then goes static. The gap runners will notice most.
  • Solo-project fragilityTwo external services (AI proxy, Garmin curl_cffi) and one Garmin WAF change from breaking sync. Bigger rivals absorb that; you carry it alone.
03

Recommended direction

Ranked for a cost-recovery hobby product with one maintainer. Play to the seams competitors cannot or will not cover. Tiers: do now, do next, hold.

Own "the runner who lifts"

Make the hybrid the headline, not a feature. Show how gym load and running load share one recovery budget — the exact cross-sport view Fitbod, Runna and intervals.icu each miss. This is your only defensible category.

Do now

Sell "no Strava required"

As Strava becomes the AI-coaching gatekeeper, make Garmin-direct access an explicit promise. Target athletes who resent the platform lock-in or its price. Free positioning, costs nothing to state.

Do now

Close the adaptation gap, narrowly

Do not build TrainAsONE. Do add one honest loop: next week's plan reacts to last week's actual load and ACWR status. "Your plan adjusts to what you did" is a claim you cannot currently make.

Do next

Make correctness the brand

Turn the domain rigor into visible trust: explain why a walk is not training, why load is all-modality. In a market of hand-wavy AI summaries, defensible honesty is a differentiator a solo dev can actually own.

Do next

Do not chase analytics depth or a social feed

Every hour spent matching intervals.icu's charts or Strava's graph is lost. You cannot win either and do not need to. Compete on scope, price, and trust instead.

Hold
The one-sentence strategy: GymCraft is not a better running analyzer or a better AI coach — those races are lost to free tools and the platform owner. It is the honest, affordable, Garmin-native home for the amateur who both lifts and runs. Everything on the roadmap should make that sentence more true.
